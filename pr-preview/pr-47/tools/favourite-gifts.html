RELATIONSHIP — FAVOURITE GIFTS

The table shows all mercenaries who have relationship progression, along with their preferred gifts and their outfit quality level (Skin Tier).

Each row represents a single mercenary and contains key information that helps you analyze their usefulness and choose the right gifts for building relationships.

The first column contains icons representing each mercenary.
Next to it is their name, which can be sorted alphabetically.

The next column shows the mercenary’s outfit — the character’s appearance in-game, which is unlocked after reaching level 20 in their relationship. Hovering over an outfit reveals details about its additional ability.

The Outfit Tier column shows the overall strength and effectiveness of a mercenary’s outfit — ranging from dominant, meta-defining units (S), through average and situational ones, down to weak (F) or not yet evaluated ones (–). Hovering over the [ i ] icon next to the column header displays descriptions for all tiers.

The last three columns show each mercenary’s preferred gifts across different rarity levels. Each tier corresponds to a different type of gift:
• Tier 1 – Rare Gifts (+12 RP)
• Tier 2 – Epic Gifts (+24 RP)
• Tier 3 – Legendary Gifts (+60 RP)
The icons in these columns indicate the specific gift variants that are best received by each character and provide the highest relationship point (RP) gains.

The table can be sorted by clicking column headers (e.g. alphabetically) and filtered by mercenary names, skin tiers, and gifts. This allows you to quickly find specific mercenaries and optimize your relationship-building strategy.
