About the Project

Small Wiki Made in My Own Way

Lust Goddess Wiki is a personal community project created to keep useful information, tools and resources in one place — not only for me, but for everyone who may find them helpful.

This is my first wiki-style website, built in my free time as a personal project that gradually grew into a community resource.

About Me

Hi, I’m Hrupec

I enjoy strategic card RPG games, and Lust Goddess caught my attention in its own way. After playing the game for a while, I started looking for useful information, guides and tools that could help me better understand the game's mechanics.

At first, the tools I created were only for my personal use. Later, the idea grew into something bigger and eventually became a complete community wiki that I decided to share with everyone.

About the Wiki

Why This Wiki Exists

While searching for information, I found many useful guides and resources, but most of them were scattered across Discord, difficult to find, or simply outdated.

Many community tools were also outdated, incomplete, or no longer worked properly. Instead of constantly searching through different sources, I decided to build the tools I wanted myself and gather everything in one place.

The Idea

How It Started

This project started from a simple need: I wanted a place with useful information and tools that would make the game easier to understand and more comfortable to play.

1.

Information was hard to find

Useful information existed, but it was often buried in Discord messages, spread across different sources, or no longer up to date.

2.

Built in my free time

I work on the wiki whenever I can. Some things may take time, but every update is made with the goal of improving the project.

3.

Made for the community

I wanted to create something useful for the community and feel like I am a small part of it by sharing tools, information and improvements.
