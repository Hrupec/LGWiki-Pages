Why Play Lust Goddess?

About the Game

You wake up in a chaotic future where the world is ruled by violence, lust and power. The city is crawling with ruthless gangs turning innocent girls into slaves, but you're not here to play nice.

As the Captain of a fierce and capable squad of mercenaries, your mission is clear: fight back, rescue the girls and earn their loyalty. Prove your dominance, gain their trust and they will reward you in ways you never imagined.

Assemble Your Dream Squad

Recruit a team of stunning mercenaries, each with their own look, skills and attitude.

Mix and match to create the perfect squad that fights hard and plays even harder.

Fight Tactical Battles

Dive into fast-paced, colorful battles where strategy meets skill.

Outsmart your enemies and prove you've got what it takes to lead.

Rescue Girls and Win Their Loyalty

Rescue captured allies from danger and they'll gladly join your squad.

Earn their loyalty and unlock unique character backstories and abilities.

Flirt, Chat and Unlock Steamy Rewards

Get closer to your companions through engaging conversations and build rapport.

Discover their secrets, send the right message, and watch the sparks fly.
