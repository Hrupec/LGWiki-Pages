Outfits

Commander Outfits Change

Commander outfits change at levels: 11, 41, 91, 121.


At every outfit change they’ll reveal a bit more of themselves.


Currently there is no option to revert these changes.


The Commanders will always show the most revealing outfit that you have unlocked.


Also, for some strange reason Ethan is the exception for this rule. He will remain fully clothed at all levels.

Ranks

Commander Ranks

Only Legendary Commanders have a Rank system and maximum rank is 15.

Ranks significantly enhance your Commander’s effectiveness on the battlefield.


As the rank increases, not only does their health improve, but new possibilities for establishing Neural Links with other commanders also emerge.


You can increase a Commander’s rank by using: and .

  • Commanders Badge — can be used to raise the rank.

  • Commander Points — can be used to increase rank.


As with Mercenaries, you can only increase your rank if you have a sufficient number of both resources. In that case, the button will become active.

The Commander Rank system is similar to the process of upgrading Mercenaries, where increasing a Commander’s rank boosts their health and unlocks new neurolinks.

Info

Keep in mind that your Commander’s base health is determined by their level, and the health gained from increasing their Rank is added to this value.

Neural Link

Commander Neural Link

Increasing a Commander’s Rank not only allows you to boost their health but also completely changes their abilities through the Neurolinks system.

When your Commander reaches the required rank, you will have the opportunity to link them with another Commander, unlock an alternative ability for the selected Commander, and enhance their health.

New abilities are unlocked gradually. The higher your Rank, the more neural connections become available to you.
