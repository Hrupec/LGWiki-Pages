Where to Play

Click on a question to show or hide the answer.

As of now, the game can be played on the following clients:

Official Site/APK
• lust-goddess.com (Browser/Android)
• lustgoddess.com (Browser/Android)
• lustyheroes (hosted by xHamster)
Nutaku.net (Browser/Android/iOS)
• xvideos.nutaku.net (hosted by xVideos)
EroLabs (Browser/Android/iOS)
Steam

! Important note: check the community Discord and official Support for how to cross-link your Steam account with the official client, and for mobile Steam advice posts.
In terms of gameplay there aren't any differences. Matches on Steam will be the same as they are on the website, and an Event will have the same difficulty on Nutaku as on EroLabs.

However, there are some small differences:

• All clients use different payment methods. See the official Support page.

• Nutaku has daily free NutakuGold (their in-between currency, essentially free money) which you can save up to buy something like a $1 deal without ever spending real money. It also regularly has deals that give out bonus NutakuGold when you buy them. This makes it a very friendly client for just about everybody. Only downside is that you can only have one account there.

• EroLabs: This client seems to receive less support than the other clients. Some features are missing with currently no plans to implement them. An incomplete list includes: no Alliance logo during Matches; no way to view the opponent's profile (for example to check their Squad); no Country Leaderboard country; and no flags or locations on player accounts.

• Steam accounts can still buy the Florence Mascot, while others could only get that one during the first month of the game. Steam is also the only client without a native mobile option for the game. Community Discord posts cover current workarounds for playing Steam accounts on mobile.
You can as long as you stay within the same client (see list in the previous question). So if you started an account on Nutaku browser, you can keep playing that same account on Nutaku mobile (Android/iOS).

Since March 3rd 2025, a single exception was made for Steam users (previously unable to play on mobile). Check the community Discord for the current how-to and troubleshooting tips.
Sadly accounts can't travel across clients. If you, for example, start an account on EroLabs, then there is no way to play that account on lust-goddess.com.

Codes and Referrals

Click on a question to show or hide the answer.

A player (the referrer) may share their profile ID (accessible by clicking your profile at the upper right corner of your main screen) to up to three other new players (referees). The referee enters the referrer's profile ID as a PromoCode (see the Promotional Codes tab below).
The referee must be between leagues 27 and 30.
Referees will get an immediate reward; referrers will get rewards based on the maximum league that the referees reach, up to league 16.
Starting from the main screen:
First go to your account info (upper left corner) and then click on the tab called 'Promotional Codes' (right side of the screen).
You can enter the codes there.
Some side notes are mentioned on the official Lust Goddess Support.

Events

Click on a question to show or hide the answer.

To be able to join the current Global Event, you need to meet a couple of requirements. These are (1) having already been in League 24 at least once, and (2) no longer be in the Newcomers Event (featuring Regina/Billie/Leta) or in the 24 hour cooldown period that follows it.

! In most cases when we receive this question, the Newcomers Event (or the cooldown following it) was still active. Once that timer runs out, you'll be able to join the rest of the game in the current Global Event.
Well, we aren't. All Global Events are difficult and take months of saving up on resources (and even then a fair bit of luck) to complete. Since the Newcomers Event is only available for brand new accounts who have not yet had the chance to save up resources (Emerix, MicroChips and MercPoints), it is considered impossible to beat.
Unless you're willing to pay thousands of dollars, but we do kinda advise against that.
For now, just look at the Newcomers Event as a training ground. It's the best place to make your first mistakes and learn from them (mostly to wait before you do something — like spinning a Roulette — until you reach an Event node that asks you to do just that).
Work through it at your own pace and see if you can beat Chapter 1. If you can do that, you're already on the right track.
And once you get stuck, don't try to force your way through. Remember: saving up resources is a very important part of this game.
Just go with the flow and enjoy your time playing!
It is hard, I agree, but not impossible!

If you're curious how people manage to do so, start with the community guides on Discord (especially the 3 Girls Event road map) and the wiki page for Three Mercenaries Events.

Couple of side notes:
• You can't complete an Event on a brand new account. To go all the way, you need a decent reserve of Emerix and MicroChips, as well as already having (most of) the featured Mercs with a large amount of MercPoints available on them. It usually takes a couple of months to collect all of that;
• A full BattlePass does help wonders as it is an excellent reserve with plenty of resources. But because there's an Event every week and the BattlePass takes three weeks to complete, you won't be able to count on that every Event. It's one of the reasons that, as a f2p account, you can only complete every 3rd or 4th Event.

Getting Stronger

Click on a question to show or hide the answer.

You can try to optimize your squad; play around with substituting mercenaries in and out of your squad. Ask yourself: which mercenaries do not have good impact, or seem to sit dead in my hand for multiple turns? Which mercenaries do I have trouble dealing with? Adjust your deck that way. Also check out the guides on Discord or ask in chat for help.
But all that will only get you so far. In the end, the level of your squad matters a lot when you are trying to improve your League.
This game is one of patience. Save up resources and only spend them when it is the best time to do so; there are many guides that tell you how to minimize spending and maximize rewards from events. Do this well and you will be on the best path to power up your squad and climb higher.
Alternatively you can always take a shortcut and spend some money!
Because being part of an Alliance provides many benefits that you can't get any other way. You could get them by creating an Alliance of your own, but being in a bigger Alliance will make getting Rewards way easier than trying to do all of it on your own.

Now, for the actual content, there are three major things that you cannot have without being part of an Alliance:
• Heli Race;
• Alliance War;
• Alliance Bonuses.

The first two (Heli Race and Alliance War) don't really focus on your Squad directly. It's a bit of a separate game and you don't have to go hard on them if you don't want to. I do think they're fun though.
Even though their main focus is on the side game, you can get some useful stuff out of them for your Squad. The main focus will be Androids. These are quite easy to unlock (mainly needs time) and provide a very big power boost to an account.

Doing the Heli Race can give you a lot of extra MercPoints at the end of each week through the Heli Crates. The Top Gear you earn for completing Heli Quests can also buy various Rewards such as Encryption Keys to unlock Androids.

The Alliance War gives a chance to win War Bonds, with which you can spin the War Spoils Roulette. Most are Rewards to get even better at Alliance War, but there's also stuff like Encryption Keys, Emerix and MicroChips in there.

And lastly, the Bonuses. There are many benefits to having access to the Alliance Bonuses.
• You need to Modify, Boost, or level up a Merc? Pop a Bonus and do it cheaper;
• Want Crates to open up faster? Pop a Bonus and they'll open faster;
• Want extra Attack on all your Ranged Mercs? Pop a Bonus!
• Want some extra Health on your Commander? You're not going to believe me, but pop that Bonus!

All in all, being part of an Alliance is a great way to get more stuff that you can't get in any other way, so give it a try!
A Reboot is basically resetting your Merc to level 1 but with an increase in their base stats. Pretty often one of their skills will also receive a boost when you do this.
These bonuses are permanent and don't cost you anything.

So is it clever to Reboot a Merc?

For Common/Rare/Epic, it generally doesn't matter, since we only use our Legendaries to fight in Matches. One exception for the Epics is when you want to pick the branch in 3 Merc Events that will ask you to have x Reboots between the featured Epics.
It does have some other uses, mainly as a way to do a couple of cheap level-ups, or as a means to influence the drop rate of certain Mercs, but that's a subject for another post.

For the Legendaries, it kinda depends from Merc to Merc if and when it’s best to Reboot.
Rule of thumb is that you don’t want to Reboot unless you can get a Merc back to the same level of Power.
For the first Reboot, that happens pretty much the moment you Reboot, so the first one is a freebie.
After that it will involve some math.
Check the community Discord Reboot Guide for the step-by-step walkthrough, and use the Level Calculator for card/credit costs.
See this table. Note there are four tables, a different one for each card rarity.
For example, if you want to get to Reboot 3 (R3), add up the cumulative costs for the R0, R1 and R2 level ups.
Legendary R7 costs are the same as R5 and R6.
Remember the credit cost as well as the card cost!

Best investment for Emerix in the long term is saving up and getting a Legendary Commander.
These become available for 4.999 Emerix every once in a while and are a pretty major boost to your account.

Second place goes to the Events.
Having a large reserve of Emerix makes it easier to complete an Event and get large amounts of Legendary Points which can once again help you tremendously.

All other investments are pretty situational.

In case you quickly need to spend a couple of hundreds, going to Alliance > Deals and getting a couple of those is a popular choice. Other options are refreshing Event Bosses, speeding up Crates, and/or refreshing Heli Quests.
There is a lot in this game that can be bought with real money. To keep this answer from being several pages long, we will assume a moderate spender with a budget of about $100. Within that budget, these are the best investments you can make.

• Buying extra Crate Slots. The rest doesn't even come close. More Crate Slots means you'll be able to do more Matches per playing session, which helps in completing Quests, most importantly the Heli Quests. The first extra Crate Slot costs $5, second one $10, third $20, and it's $50 for the last one. So $85 in total. The Rewards you get with them do scale by your Max League (furthest you've ever gotten), so it's best to wait with the last two until you're a bit further in the game.

• Some of the $1 Deals are very interesting as well. The most generous ones are those available under 'Deals' during Seasonal Recruits. Because these are only available for players that didn't purchase anything else before, it can be worth it to wait buying anything else if one of these is expected to happen soon.

• Next best thing is the BattlePass Premium ($20). Once again, the Rewards scale with your Max League. The only exception is the very first one (fixed Rewards) so don't buy the first BattlePass Premium! From the second one on, whenever you feel like you'll be able to complete the BattlePass, buy it at the very start. I do say at the very start, because once bought, it'll automatically open every Premium Crate that can be unlocked. Buying it at the start makes sure that you can choose and pick what to open when.

• There's also the Monthly Card that is pretty interesting. It gives out the most Emerix per dollar spent.

Everything else you can pretty much ignore if you want to stay within the $100 budget.

If your budget is bigger than this, please seek advice in the general chats.

Every once in a while (roughly once a month) there is a special Event where one of the Legendary Commanders becomes available for 4.999 Emerix. During this time there will also be Quests to win Matches with that one Commander.
Completing these offer a chance to earn back a small portion of those Emerix. These Quests are also available for those that bought the same Commander at an earlier time.

You can find more information on Commander's Path and Commanders.
The War Echo Event is an irregular mini-event. In other words, it's not always available and it's very hard to predict when it will become available once again.
A little more information about what the mini-event is about can be found on War Echo.

! Please note that the War Echo mini-event only has a very small chance of unlocking new War Troops. Please prepare yourself mentally — this is a type of Event that will give you some free stuff. You will benefit. But you will, most likely, not unlock a new War Troop. Please keep this in mind when discussing War Echo.

Most of the Troops can be bought with Turbo Cores. A couple of them can only be unlocked during War Echo. During this time there will be a number of Quests that can be completed for Tokens with which you can spin the War Echo Roulette. Some of the possible Rewards on that Roulette are the locked Troops. Since it is a Roulette, please be warned that unlocking them isn't guaranteed.

Please note that none of the War Echo Quests require War Bonds. The Quests are to level up Troops, deploy Troops, spend Apex, and complete Heli Quests. There is no benefit to saving up War Bonds for this Event.
There are three types of Recruits, each with their own nuances when it comes to availability, cost and usefulness.

Temptation Soldiers:
• First spin = 149 Emerix
• Second spin = 139 Emerix
• All spins after those = 129 Emerix

Notes:
1. You need to stay within the Recruit screen to benefit from the discounts. Leave the screen = reset the count.
2. If you start by using MicroChips (or the once a week free Recruit), the count also goes up. So if one only has 1 MicroChip, first uses their free Recruit and then uses the MicroChip = the first spin for Emerix will cost 129 Emerix.

Seasonal Recruits:
• First spin = 499 Emerix
• Second spin = 479 Emerix
• All spins after those = 449 Emerix

Notes:
1. Same number 1 as before;
2. Same number 2 as before, minus MicroChips (can't use those there) and there's a free Seasonal Recruit once every 24 hours, instead of once a week.

Lust Goddesses:
• First spin = 4.499 Emerix
• Second spin = what are you doing?
• All spins after those = please stop!

Notes:
1. I think same number 1 as before?
2. I think same number 2 as before, but without free spins, and with QuantumChips instead of MicroChips?
