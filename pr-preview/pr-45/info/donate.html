Before You Donate

Is donating required?

No. The wiki is free to use. Donations are optional and only help with yearly hosting costs.

Where does the money go?

Funds are used to keep the website online, cover hosting and domain costs, and maintain the wiki project.

Can I stay anonymous?

Yes. Supporters can be listed by nickname or stay anonymous.
